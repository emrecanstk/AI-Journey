# Linear Algebra Basics

## [EN]

### What I learned

- Vectors represent numerical information.
- Matrices are collections of vectors.
- Dot product combines corresponding values and produces a score.

### AI connection

Dot products sit under similarity and Attention.
Q × K → score is the same idea as a · b.

### Experiment

A NumPy dot product: `[1, 2, 3] · [4, 5, 6] = 32`.

Today we do not go into matrix multiplication as a separate topic.
`Q @ K.T` is many dot products stacked; that is enough.

## [TUR]

### What I learned

- Vector, sayısal bilgiyi temsil eder.
- Matrix, vektörlerin bir araya gelmiş halidir.
- Dot product, karşılıklı değerleri çarpıp bir skor üretir.

Dot product, similarity ve Attention'ın altında durur.

Bugün matrix multiplication'a ayrı konu olarak girmiyoruz.
